Bogota's Ajiaco

Bogota's Ajiaco is a traditional Colombian potato soup made with three types of potatoes, chicken, and a unique local herb called guasas

Ingredients

  • 2 Chicken breasts
  • 3 Types of potatoes (2 lbs Papa Criolla/yellow, 1 lb Pastusa, 1 lb Sabanera), peeled and sliced
  • 2 Ears of corn (cut into pieces)
  • 1 green onion stalk & 2 garlic cloves (chopped)
  • To serve: Heavy cream, capers, and avocado slices

Instructions

  1. Boil chicken with water, corn, green onion, garlic, and salt until tender
  2. Add the three types of potatoes to the broth [1]. Cook until the Criolla potatoes dissolve completely to thicken the soup
  3. Return the shredded chicken to the pot [1]. Add the guasas and simmer for 5–10 minutes to infuse the flavor
  4. Pour into bowls, ensuring each gets a piece of corn [1]. Top with heavy cream and capers, and serve with avocado on the side

    5. Your ajiaco is ready to be enjoyed!
