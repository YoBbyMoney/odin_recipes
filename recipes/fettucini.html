Fetuccini with Tomato Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fettuccini pasta
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Cook the fettuccini pasta according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomato sauce to the skillet and simmer for 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  4. Toss the cooked fettuccini pasta with the tomato sauce until well coated.
  5. Serve the pasta topped with grated Parmesan cheese. Enjoy!
