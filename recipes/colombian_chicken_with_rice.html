Colombian Chicken with rice

Colombian Arroz con Pollo is a one-pot dish featuring shredded chicken, rice, and mixed vegetables cooked in a flavorful tomato-onion seasoning called hogao.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • White rice
  • Chicken broth
  • Mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion and 2 tomatoes
  • 1 Red bell pepper
  • 1 tbsp achiote powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt,pepper, and cumin

Instructions

  1. Boil chicken with garlic, onion, and salt. Shred the meat. Save the broth.
  2. Cook rice using 4 cups of the saved chicken broth and the achiote powder
  3. Sauté the onion, tomatoes, and bell pepper with cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stir the chicken, peas, carrots, and green beans into the sauce. Cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Fold the chicken and vegetable mixture into the cooked rice until completely uniform.
  6. Serve hot with potato chips and avocado slices.
